# Spend Journal – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Spend Journal is developed by Moritz Martin.
Contact: doublemlabs1@gmail.com

## Summary
Spend Journal works offline. Your financial data stays on your device and does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## Data stored on your device
Everything you enter in Spend Journal (transactions, categories, budgets, recurring entries, notes and receipt photos) is stored only on your device. Nobody else has access to it.

## Backups and exports
When you create a backup or a CSV export, the file is saved to the location you choose (for example your device storage or a cloud folder such as Google Drive). Once saved there, the file is handled by that storage provider under its own privacy policy. Spend Journal does not send these files anywhere else.

Spend Journal also uses Android's built-in backup: your Spend Journal data (without receipt photos) is backed up to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling. This only happens if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, and only when the backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock, so neither Google nor Spend Journal can read it. When you transfer directly from one phone to another, receipt photos are copied as well.

## App lock
If you enable the app lock, authentication (fingerprint, face or device PIN) is handled entirely by Android. Spend Journal never receives or stores biometric data.

## No tracking
Spend Journal contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Your rights
Since Spend Journal does not process your personal data, there is nothing for Spend Journal to access, correct or delete. You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. If you have questions, contact me at the email address above.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
